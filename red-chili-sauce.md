## Red Chili Sauce

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ocUVI4VjSeQ


A selection of dried chilies.

* I usually do half [Gaujillo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guajillo_chili)
* And the other half Passila Ancho or mix of what's on hand 

Remove seeds.

Pan toast them. Don't burn.

Put them in water for 20 min with an onion and some (3-9) cloves of garlic.

Blend.

Salt and taste.

Put it through a sieve.
