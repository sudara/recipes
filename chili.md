## Chili

There are chili contests all over the US for a reason. It's a very flexible dish and everyone has a different take.

I make it differently each time.

I like beans in my chili. Deal with it. 

Usually I have some leftover pinto beans I'll throw in there. I'll almost always put kidney beans in.

### The key

The keys are, in my opinion:

* Complexity and depth of flavor. Add garlic. But also garlic powder. Add a couple different kinds of onions if you have em.
* Something smokey. Bacon, smoked paprika, liquid smoke, chipotle all work.
* Complexity of spice. I also like red chili sauce as a base. It's worth the extra 30 min. I also
  always use fresh (chopped frozen) jalapeños.

### Ingredients

For one big pot:

* 700-1kg Meat. I'm lazy and usually use ground beef.
* Beans, at least two varieties. I use 2 cans of kidney + leftover pinto or you could use white beans.
* Cumin
* 2 big onions
* 1 bulb of garlic (8+ cloves)
* 3-6 cups of chicken stock. This will depend on if you are using bean water, red chili sauce, etc.
* can of crushed tomatos

Optional Ingredients

* Bacon or some smokey pork
* Worcestershire sauce
* Granulated onion/garlic
* Chipotle in adobo

Optional toppings:

* Green onions
* Cheddar
* Sour cream
* Tortilla chips

### Red chili

* toast various peppers, including guajillo, chipotle, ancho
* de-seed them
* cook for 15 min in 2 cups of water with a coarse cut onion and some soup cube

### Cook:

Brown meat. Get some color on it.

Add optional bacon or smoked meat.

You want the meat to fry it its own oil.

Add Onions, garlic, soften for a few min.

Deglaze with Chicken stock and optional red chili sauce.

Add granulated onion and garlic, worstershire sauce, cumin, jalapeno, oregano.

Simmer to desired consistency for at least 30 min.

Add toppings.
