# Sticky Toffee Pudding

### Ingredients

* 170g dates, chopped
* 1 cup water for boiling
* 1.5 cups flour
* 1 tsp baking soda
* 2 eggs
* 80g butter
* nutmeg
* vanilla

sauce:
* 1 cup brown sugar
* 1/2 cup (113g) butter, softened
* 1/3 cup golden syrup
* 2 tbsp lyles treacle
* 2 cups heavy cream
* vanilla
* salt

* Grease 9x9 pan. Preheat 180C
* Chop dates small
* Bring water to boil with dates, simmer on low for 5 min
* Bowl with flour, baking soda, salt, nutmeg
* In another bowl: whip butter
* add sugar + beat until light
* add eggs + vanilla
* put half of the flour -> butter mixture
* bake 30-40 min, until poked skewer comes out clean
* poke with holes

make sauce:
* combine sugar, syrups + butter and reduce for 2 min
* add cream, boil for 4 min
* cook on low for 20 min until amber caramel color
* add remaining cream
* cut heat, add vanilla and salt

Pour 1/2 the sauce over cake, put back in oven until sauce bubbles
